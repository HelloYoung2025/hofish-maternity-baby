This separate privacy page identifies www.hofish.net and Shopify. Its published text contains template instructions and blanks.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/policies/privacy-policy>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Device, order and support information

- The text names www.hofish.net and lists browser and device details, IP and time zone, browsing and search activity, and order details such as payment, billing, delivery and contact information. It leaves the scope of support data and other sources partly blank.
- It describes Shopify’s role in processing information and possible sharing with legal authorities. Uses include site operation, analytics, checkout, fulfillment, fraud screening and updates or marketing aligned with stated preferences. Provider lists and the minimum age are unfinished.

## Advertising, cookies and browser choices

- The template has conditional passages for analytics and targeted advertising. It includes opt-out examples for Facebook, Google, Bing and the Digital Advertising Alliance while leaving the exact services in use unresolved.
- Cookie tables describe functions for cart and checkout, storefront sessions, login, account changes, analytics and marketing referrals. The page gives 30 minutes to two years as the range for most persistent cookies, notes that blocking can affect access and says it does not change its practices based on Do Not Track.

## Retention, fraud screening and European data

- The retention clause describes keeping order information until an erasure request. The European template lists potential grounds including consent, performance of a contract and legal obligations without selecting which apply.
- The page describes temporary fraud screening by Shopify after failed transactions. It leaves HOFISH’s own choice about significant automated decisions blank. The European rights passage describes processing first in Ireland and transfers outside Europe, including to Canada and the United States.

## Rights, complaints and gaps in the published text

- The European and California passages discuss access, correction, erasure and portability. The California section also discusses appointing a representative. The complaint section gives a mailing address in Diamond Bar, California, but leaves the email, update date and supervisory-authority details as placeholders.
- A conditional template block describes sales of personal information but omits categories, opt-out instructions, rules for minors and incentives. The footer policy says HOFISH does not plan to sell data. The two documents leave actual practices unclear.
