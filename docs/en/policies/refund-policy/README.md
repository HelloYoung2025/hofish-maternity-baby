The separate Shopify refund policy allows requests within 30 days of receipt. The storefront footer version gives a different starting date and contact.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/policies/refund-policy>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Request and eligibility

- Start the request at contact@hofish.net within 30 days of receiving the item. The Shopify page requires the original condition, tags and packaging, with the product unworn or unused and accompanied by a receipt or other purchase evidence.
- For an approved request, the page describes a return label and directions for the address and shipping procedure. Returns sent before a request is made are ineligible under this version. The text gives no mailing address or postage price.

## Damage, defects and order errors

- The page asks customers to inspect the shipment on arrival and report damage, a defect or the wrong item immediately so the store can assess the issue. The footer policy specifies a 72-hour window for damaged or defective goods, while this page uses an immediate reporting instruction.

## Excluded products

- The published exclusions include discounted merchandise, gift cards, perishables, personalized or special-order items and personal-care goods. It also rules out hazardous materials, flammable liquids and gases.

## Changing items and refund status

- For a different item, the page describes requesting a return, waiting for acceptance, then placing a new purchase separately.
- After inspecting the return received, the store says it will notify you of the decision. An approved refund goes back automatically to the original method. A bank or card issuer may take additional time to show the credit, and this page gives no fixed posting deadline.
