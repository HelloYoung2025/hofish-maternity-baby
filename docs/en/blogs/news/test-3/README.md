Published March 30, 2023, this article explains how the flange sits against the breast and why its fit can vary from person to person.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/blogs/news/test-3>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Included flanges and fit

- A pump may come with a flange sized for the product. The article points out that a size included in the box may fit some users better than others.

## Signs of a comfortable fit

- The article describes a nipple that rests in the centre of the tunnel and moves without rubbing. It also describes comfort after the session and a breast that feels less full.

## Signs to check the fit

- The post lists pain, redness, discolouration, bruising, excess tissue drawn into the tunnel and a breast that still feels full. It also mentions blocked ducts among its examples. A lactation professional can help assess persistent discomfort.

## Two measuring tools

- The article describes using a millimetre ruler and using a gauge with round openings. Its ruler technique measures from base to tip. Flange sizing can use a different measurement, so use the instructions for your device.

## Examples of sizes

- The source uses 24 mm as an example of a common included size. It names 17, 19, 21, 24 and 27 mm in a section that links to Momcozy's guide and products.
