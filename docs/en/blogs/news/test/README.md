# Breastfeeding with flat or inverted nipples

Published March 30, 2023, the article looks at how nipple shape and breast fullness can affect latch as parent and baby learn to breastfeed.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/blogs/news/test>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Identifying nipple shape

- The post describes how flat and inverted nipples may appear when the tissue around them is gently compressed. It notes that learning to latch can take time and can differ from one baby to another.

## Nipple stimulation

- The post discusses touch, a cool cloth and the Hoffman technique as ways to draw the nipple outward. It says nipples may protrude more readily over time with feeding or pumping.
- For breast or nipple stimulation during pregnancy, ask your maternity team about your circumstances.

## Hand expression

- The article describes hand expression to soften a very full breast and make it easier for the baby to attach. It links to a commercial massage device.

## Using a breast pump

- The source describes pump suction as another approach and promotes Momcozy's S9 Pro and S12 Pro wearable pumps.

## Other aids

- Its other examples are small suction cups, latch aids with a bulb, plunger-style devices and breast shells that press on surrounding tissue.

## Nipple shields

- The article describes silicone shields as an aid worn over the nipple during a feed. It raises concerns about milk transfer and about a baby becoming accustomed to feeding with a shield.

## Lactation support

- A lactation professional can observe a feed and help decide whether an aid fits your situation.
