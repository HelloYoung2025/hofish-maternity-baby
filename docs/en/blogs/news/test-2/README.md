Published March 30, 2023, this first-person account covers physical recovery, learning to breastfeed and finding support during nights with a newborn.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/blogs/news/test-2>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Healing after childbirth

- The writer had second-degree perineal tears that needed stitches after both vaginal births. A postpartum nurse explained wound care before the writer went home.
- The writer recalls a rinse bottle, pads, topical products and a sitz bath as parts of a personal routine. Ask your maternity team which forms of care are appropriate for your recovery.

## Learning to breastfeed

- The writer describes early pain and nipple injury. A hospital lactation consultant and other parents helped, and feeding became less painful over the first weeks.
- The article discusses taking a breastfeeding class and promotes Momcozy products. It also acknowledges that families may stop breastfeeding earlier than planned.

## Support through long nights

- The writer told their partner how lonely overnight feeding felt. The partner then stayed with them during night feeds.
- After the second birth, exchanging messages with friends who also had newborns helped the writer feel supported. The piece closes by encouraging parents to seek preparation and company.
