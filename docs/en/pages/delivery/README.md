The separate HOFISH Delivery page publishes three shipping methods for maternity wear. Other HOFISH shipping pages show different prices and estimated timeframes.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/pages/delivery>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Shipping methods and published estimates

- Standard shipping is USD 5.99, with estimated arrival in 6–12 business days. The table shows a processing estimate of 1–2 business days.
- Express shipping is USD 8.99, with estimated arrival in 3–4 business days. The processing estimate is 1–2 business days.
- Priority shipping is USD 15.99, with estimated arrival in 1–3 business days. The processing estimate is 1–2 business days.

## Packing and processing

- The page says packages are prepared and dispatched Monday through Friday and most ship within 48 hours after ordering.
- Weekend and selected holiday orders are processed within the next two business days. Missing or inaccurate payment information may add two business days. Out-of-stock items may add five business days.

## What determines the charge and the carrier

- The order subtotal before tax and shipping is used to calculate the rate. HOFISH says it chooses the carrier according to the delivery address.
- The footer's Shipping Delivery page instead quotes destination-specific charges and a 3–5 business day transit estimate. The alternate Shipping policy gives another timeline. Review the shipping price and timing offered at checkout.
