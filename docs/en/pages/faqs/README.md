This guide answers common HOFISH catalog and order questions using the relevant official product and support pages. The original FAQ body contains golf club template copy.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/pages/faqs>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Where do I find products and sizing?

- The catalog groups nursing bras, hands-free pumping bras, nursing camisoles, maternity underwear and bottoms. Each item shows its listed options and any fabric, care or fitting details available from its source.
- The general Size Chart page does not show a table. The size guide gives a model-specific example for the Deep-V seamless nursing bra three-pack. Check the exact product before comparing sizes across styles.

## How can I check if my pump fits a bra?

- Open the exact pumping bra page and check how it holds the flange or wearable pump. The designs include overlapping fabric panels and zip openings.
- Compatibility depends on the bra and pump setup. Check any model or flange names the product page gives and contact HOFISH if you need confirmation.

## Where is my parcel?

- Use the tracking number from the shipping notice on the official Order tracking page. For a missing tracking number, contact HOFISH with the order reference.

## Which delivery or return terms apply?

- Delivery and shipping pages give different rates and timings. Check the shipping options shown at checkout for your destination.
- The footer return page and the separate Shopify policy use different instructions and date references. Ask HOFISH which one applies before sending an item back.

## How can I reach HOFISH?

- Email contact@hofish.net, call the telephone number on the Contact page, or use the official store form. For an HF19HY mattress, see the dedicated recall page.
