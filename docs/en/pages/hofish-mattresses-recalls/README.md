# HOFISH mattress recall

[English](<README.md>) · [Deutsch](<../../../de/pages/hofish-mattresses-recalls/README.md>) · [Français](<../../../fr/pages/hofish-mattresses-recalls/README.md>)

[Home](<../../README.md>)

Stop using an affected mattress immediately. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) issued recall 21-097 on March 17, 2021. The affected mattresses failed the mandatory U.S. flammability standard and pose a fire hazard.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/pages/hofish-mattresses-recalls>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Identify the affected product

- The recall covers the HOFISH 10-inch hybrid foam and pocket-spring mattress in Twin, Twin XL, Full, Queen and King. The mattresses were compressed and sold in a box.
- Look for a single-sided mattress with a white quilted top, a blue wave pattern, gray sides and white piping. Use the prototype number on the label to confirm the recall.
- Check the white label on the underside at the head of the mattress. It identifies HOFISH Inc. and lists the prototype number HF19HY. Only that prototype is included.

## Arrange the free repair

- Call HOFISH toll-free at 888-731-2419, Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. U.S. Mountain Time, or email frank@hofish.net.
- Request the fitted cover that HOFISH supplies at no charge to bring the mattress into compliance. The published CPSC notice shows the mattress and both sides of the label.
- If there is a problem getting the remedy, use the recall complaint form reached from the CPSC notice.

## Sale and production details

- The CPSC reports about 700 units sold exclusively through Amazon.com from September 2019 through January 2020 at about USD 190.
- Vietnam Glory Home Furnishings Joint Stock Company manufactured the mattresses in Vietnam. HOFISH Inc. of Diamond Bar, California, was the importer.
- The CPSC notice recorded no reported injuries or incidents at the time of publication.

## Which date and reference number to use

- HOFISH's page displays a draft dated January 21, 2021. Use the final CPSC notice dated March 17, 2021 and reference number 21-097.
