HOFISH’s posted website terms address access, user submissions and disputes.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/pages/terms-of-use>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Access, age and permitted use

- The site terms set a minimum age of 18 and require authority to accept them. Access is limited to revocable, personal, noncommercial use.
- HOFISH and its suppliers reserve their intellectual-property rights. The text restricts republication, commercial exploitation, modification, reverse engineering and use to build a competing site. It allows changes or suspension without notice and promises no website maintenance or support.

## Submissions, feedback and conduct

- Authors bear responsibility for their posts and for keeping copies. The terms give HOFISH an irrevocable worldwide license, including sublicensing, for using those posts on the site. They separately assign rights in suggestions and treat them as nonconfidential.
- The acceptable-use rules address infringement, abusive or deceptive content, spam, harvesting user data, unauthorized access and automated requests. Enforcement may include editing or removing content, closing an account or notifying authorities. Users may have to cover certain third-party claims arising from their use, breaches or submissions.

## Third parties, cookies and releases

- External advertisements and sites follow their own terms and privacy practices. The text places responsibility for user posts and interactions on users, includes a broad release of site-related claims and invokes California Civil Code section 1542 for California residents.
- The cookie paragraph identifies browsing history and preferences as inputs for customizing the site.

## Warranties, liability and termination

- For the site, the text disclaims warranties and limits any warranties required by law to 90 days from first use where allowed. It excludes specified losses and gives a USD 50 aggregate cap, subject to local law.
- Termination ends access immediately and can lead to deletion of account content. The text identifies provisions that continue after termination.

## Copyright complaints

- The copyright section describes removal and account termination for repeated infringement. It calls for a signed notice identifying the work, the disputed material and its location, contact information and declarations of good faith, accuracy and authority. It refers to 17 U.S.C. § 512 and warns that material misrepresentations can carry liability.

## Notice and individual arbitration

- The text gives 16830 Ventura Blvd., Suite 360, Encino, CA 91436 for written dispute notices. A notice identifies the claim and requested remedy. After 30 days from receipt without resolution, the text directs covered disputes to individual arbitration in English before one neutral arbitrator, normally through the American Arbitration Association.
- For claims below USD 10,000 the initiating party can choose a procedure without in-person attendance. At USD 10,000 or above, the arbitration rules determine hearing rights. The terms discuss hearing location, filing limits, confidentiality, cost sharing and a payment floor of USD 2,500 if the award beats the company’s last qualifying settlement offer.

## Exceptions, changes and contact

- The terms include jury and class-action waivers, with routes for individual small claims and urgent interim relief. They list defamation, certain computer-law claims and specified intellectual-property disputes as outside arbitration. The court venue appears in the source as “Netherlands County, California,” which the text does not clarify.
- The terms describe electronic notices, a 30-calendar-day period for substantial changes for existing users, and immediate effect for new users. They address export controls, severability and assignment, and list warranty@hofish.net as the general terms contact.
