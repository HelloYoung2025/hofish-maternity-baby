The HOFISH page linked in the store footer sets out handling, transit estimates, country-based prices and destination restrictions. Confirm the rate and available services at checkout.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/pages/shipping-delivery>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Processing and the delivery address

- The stated handling time is one to two business days. The estimate excludes preorders.
- Verify the full delivery address before dispatch. The page says HOFISH cannot redirect the parcel after it is on its way.

## Transit estimates

- The published estimate is three to five business days in transit, excluding weekends and holidays. It allows up to 20 business days for rural destinations.
- Handling adds a separate one to two business days. An invalid address, customs clearance or other events can extend the estimate.

## Prices by destination

- For an order under USD 24.90, the table shows USD 4.99 for the United States, USD 7.50 for Canada and USD 4.99 for Germany. Each row gives three to five business days.
- The footer advertises free shipping over USD 24.90. The shipping page says rates may change and also sets out destination restrictions.

## Express, customs and country restrictions

- The page excludes P.O. boxes and U.S. APO/FPO military addresses from express shipping. It says the customer pays customs duties.
- The source lists restrictions for many destinations, including France, the United Kingdom and Japan. It describes the list as non-exhaustive and subject to change. Consult the original list and check what service is available for your destination.

## How the other pages differ

- The separate Delivery page gives standard, express and priority prices of USD 5.99, USD 8.99 and USD 15.99. The alternate Shipping policy estimates 3–5 business days for fulfillment plus 7–10 for transit. The footer's destination table and the checkout options are separate references.
