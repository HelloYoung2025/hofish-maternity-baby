The HOFISH footer policy describes how the brand says it handles information from browsing, purchases, support and social media.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/pages/privacy-policy>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Data collected when customers visit or buy

- The policy covers contact, shipping, account and demographic details supplied through purchases, account registration or communications. It separately describes automatic device and browsing data, limited payment records and public profile information from social networks.
- Providing personal details is described as voluntary, but declining can prevent purchases or access to features. The policy directs users to social-platform privacy settings for limiting profile access.

## Purposes and grounds for processing

- HOFISH describes fulfilling orders, answering requests, developing products, conducting research and audits, running promotions and protecting customers. It cites legitimate interests, consent that can be withdrawn, and legal duties.
- The marketing section discusses prior express consent for marketing emails, selected partner campaigns and personalization or measurement across third-party sites. Promotions can have additional data terms.

## Sharing, public posts and advertising

- Listed recipients and uses include business processors, advertisers, investors or other parties conducting analysis, fraud prevention and responding to legal demands. HOFISH also discusses insurance, risk management and protecting or defending its rights.
- Comments and profile details can be visible to other users. The policy says online comments may be distributed outside the site, and third-party advertising services apply their own privacy settings and policies.

## Cookies, pixels and email tracking

- The text covers server logs, essential and preference cookies, social and advertising cookies, pixel tags and email-open confirmations. It gives examples for sign-in, remembering settings, personalization, analytics and targeted ads.
- The described controls include a first-visit consent prompt and browser settings. The policy warns that disabling cookies can affect purchases or other site functions.

## Security, minors and sensitive information

- HOFISH describes safeguarding data, using online payment security measures and notifying users promptly of known breaches that could expose them to serious risk. The policy describes limits to transmission security and online exposure.
- The policy describes the website and apps as intended for ages 16 and over and asks readers to report under-16 data for deletion. It also asks readers to avoid submitting sensitive information such as identity numbers, health or genetic data.

## Requests, California and newsletter choices

- The policy discusses confirmation and access, a free first digital copy, portability, deletion, restrictions, withdrawal of consent and email opt-outs. It describes identity checks, possible fees for further copies and exceptions to deletion.
- For California, the text describes verified access and deletion requests, including verification of residency. It lists identifiers, commercial and browsing information, comments, location, employment and education as possible categories. It says HOFISH does not plan to sell data.
- The newsletter section describes unsubscribing, possible retention of former subscriber records and sharing for ordinary marketing operations. The separate Shopify privacy template includes conflicting conditional sale language and leaves its choices incomplete.
