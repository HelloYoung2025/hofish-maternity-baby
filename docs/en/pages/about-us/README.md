HOFISH describes itself as a maternity and baby brand founded in 2016. Its About page introduces the brand through its work on everyday comfort for mothers in the United States.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/pages/about-us>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Brand focus

- The brand says it designs for the ordinary routines of pregnancy, nursing and caring for a baby. It describes style and comfort as priorities alongside product quality.
- Its goal in the brand statement is to make these products available to more mothers.

## Maternity products and the store catalog

- The About page names nursing and pumping bras, nursing tops, maternity shorts and breast pumps. The documented catalog also includes maternity underwear, leggings and jeans.
- HOFISH's brand description also mentions feeding accessories, children's toys and stroller organizers. The About page describes the brand range, and the catalog shows the individual products documented here.

## How HOFISH develops products

- HOFISH says its development team collects customers' observations and ideas, then revises design details to address day-to-day needs.
- The brand highlights word-of-mouth recommendations for some of its nursing bras. That description comes from HOFISH's own About page.
