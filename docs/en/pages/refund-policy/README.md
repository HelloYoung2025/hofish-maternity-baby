This is the refund page linked in the storefront footer. Its timing and instructions differ from those on the separate Shopify refund policy. The storefront’s “7 Days Guarantee” badge links to this policy without defining a separate return window.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/pages/refund-policy>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Order cancellations

- The footer policy gives customers 48 hours after ordering to request cancellation. It estimates another 3–5 business days for the cancellation refund to appear on the original payment method.

## Return window and item condition

- This page measures the 30-day return and exchange window from the purchase date. Its partial-refund paragraph separately mentions goods sent back more than 30 days after delivery. The page leaves the relationship between those deadlines unclear.
- It requires a receipt or other proof of purchase and asks for unused goods in the condition and packaging in which they arrived. It excludes some health and personal-care items and says discounted items do not qualify for refunds.
- A partial refund may apply when a return lacks parts, is damaged or no longer in its original condition for reasons outside the store’s responsibility.

## Return shipping

- The page directs customers to support@hofish-maternity.com for return arrangements. It lists Amazon.com Services, Inc., 33333 LBJ FWY, Dallas, TX 75241-7203, United States, and tells customers to avoid shipping returns to the manufacturer. Confirm the address with the support team before sending anything.
- The customer pays return postage, and the policy excludes those charges from the refund. For goods worth more than USD 75, it suggests tracking or shipping insurance and states that receipt of the package is unguaranteed.

## Refund decisions and delays

- The policy describes an email once the return arrives and is examined, followed by an approval or rejection. Approved amounts are credited to the same payment method. It gives a specific processing estimate for cancellations only.
- For a missing credit, it asks the customer to check the account and contact the card issuer and bank before escalating to support@hofish-maternity.com.

## Defective or damaged products

- The page limits replacements to damaged or defective products and requires contacting support@hofish-maternity.com within 72 hours of delivery. How long a replacement takes to arrive depends on the destination.

## Gifts

- For a purchase designated as a gift and delivered directly to the recipient, an accepted return leads to gift credit sent to that recipient. The page describes a mailed certificate.
- When the purchase was unmarked as a gift or went to the purchaser to give later, the policy sends the refund to that purchaser.

## Compare the published versions

- The separate Shopify refund policy counts 30 days from receipt, uses contact@hofish.net and describes a shipping label for accepted returns. Confirm which process applies to the order.
