This alternate shipping page publishes estimates and store names that differ from those in the footer’s Shipping Delivery page. Check the delivery terms shown for your destination at checkout.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/pages/shipping-policy>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Order preparation and transit

- This page advertises free worldwide shipping without identifying the carrier. It gives 3–5 working days on average to prepare the order and another 7–10 business days in transit after dispatch, depending on the destination.
- It says holidays, natural disasters and pandemics may affect the estimates. The storefront footer instead advertises free shipping above USD 24.90, while its separate Shipping Delivery page gives other destination rates and shorter delivery estimates.

## Preorders and backorders

- If an order combines items with different availability, the page says available stock ships first and preordered or backordered goods follow once ready. A first delivery may contain only part of the order.

## Tracking

- This page describes a confirmation message by email or, when a mobile number is provided, by text. The message includes a tracking number and link. It gives 1–3 working days for tracking data to appear and also directs buyers to the Order Tracking page.

## Correct a delivery address

- The page makes the buyer responsible for the address supplied at checkout. It directs address corrections to support@hofish-maternity.com immediately because orders move quickly into processing.

## Returns and missing packages

- The returns paragraph names Top Trend Store, assigns return shipping to the buyer and refers to the refund policy for the process. The package paragraph names Best Trends Online Store and states that store excludes responsibility for loss or theft.
- When the carrier marks a package delivered and the recipient cannot locate it, the page directs the recipient to the carrier shown in tracking. It gives support@hofish-maternity.com for help with the process. Confirm the applicable terms and delivery charge with HOFISH because of the different store names and published rates.
