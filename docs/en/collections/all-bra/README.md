## Nursing bras for everyday wear and movement

- The direct-nursing styles range from a full-coverage three-pack to a deep-V three-pack and a triple-clip two-pack. The racerback sports bra adds removable pads and nursing clips.

## Compare pumping access

- Pumping models use overlapping fabric, cross-shaped openings or adjustable zippers to hold flanges. On the zip styles, the cups open separately for breastfeeding.
- If adjustment matters, compare the strap settings, hook-and-eye positions and any included band extender in the individual listings.
