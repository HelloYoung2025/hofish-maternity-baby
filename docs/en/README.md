## What HOFISH sells

- Nursing bras come in seamless, wire-free everyday and sports styles. The range includes full-coverage and deep-V designs, with drop-down cups and multipacks on selected styles.
- Hands-free pumping bras hold pump flanges in place. Depending on the model, access is through overlapping fabric panels or zip openings. Several styles also have drop-down cups for breastfeeding.
- Nursing camisoles combine stretch fabric with built-in support and clip-down access. Seamless maternity briefs have a high waistband that covers the bump.
- Maternity leggings, skinny jeans and shorts use stretchy over-bump waistbands or panels. The range includes stretch-denim shorts and biker shorts with side pockets.

## Sustainability goals

- Manufacturing. HOFISH identifies responsible production as a priority.
- Durability. The brand says it aims to design items for longer use.
- Carbon footprint. The homepage names reducing emissions as a goal.
- Materials. HOFISH aims to source materials more sustainably. The homepage provides no item-level certifications or quantified progress for these four goals.

- HOFISH frames its product design around making nursing more comfortable and easier to manage, with clothing intended for pregnancy and the months after birth.
- The brand introduction describes a gap between useful nursing products, everyday comfort and affordability. The About Us page gives the wider story.
