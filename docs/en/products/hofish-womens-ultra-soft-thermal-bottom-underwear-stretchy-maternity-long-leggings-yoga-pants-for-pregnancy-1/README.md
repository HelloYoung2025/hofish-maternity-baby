## Design and pockets

- These pull-on biker shorts have a high, flexible waistband that extends over the bump. The listing describes four-way stretch and a fabric designed to be breathable and opaque.
- Two deep side pockets hold small items such as a phone, cards or keys. HOFISH presents the shorts for activities such as walking, running and yoga as well as daily wear.

## Fabric and conflicting care directions

- The listed composition is 80% polyester and 20% spandex.
- One line says hand wash only, while the description also mentions machine washing with similar colors. It further advises avoiding bleach and hot soaking. Follow the garment's care label and contact the retailer if the label is unclear.

## Colors and sizes

- Black appears in sizes Small through XX-Large.
- The measurements printed in the description refer to 2018 and 2019 full-length leggings. They do not establish the waist or length of these shorts. Confirm the shorts sizing before ordering if you need those measurements.
