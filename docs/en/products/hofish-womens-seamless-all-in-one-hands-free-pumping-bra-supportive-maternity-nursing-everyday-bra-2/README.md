An everyday maternity, nursing and pumping bra with layered cups and no front zipper.

[View source page](<https://www.hofish.com/products/hofish-womens-seamless-all-in-one-hands-free-pumping-bra-supportive-maternity-nursing-everyday-bra-2>) · Source snapshot 2026-11-20

## Layered cup design

- A shoulder clip releases the outer nursing cup. For hands-free pumping, the opening between two fabric panels holds the flange against the body. HOFISH shows wearables fitted inside the cups as a second use case.
- The front has no zipper. The page gives no compatibility list for individual pump models. Check whether the flange or wearable device sits securely with your chosen size.

## Materials and adjustability

- The product description lists 92% nylon and 8% spandex. The wire-free, seamless knit has full cups and a U-shaped back.
- The shoulder straps adjust separately, and the hook-and-eye back has four positions. An extender described on the page adds up to 2 in (about 5 cm) to the band.

## Single and two-pack choices

- The selector offers M, L and XL. It shows single black and beige bras and two-packs in black or black/beige.
- The description states one bra with a matching extender for the single option. Confirm the accessory count for each two-pack with the store. The page directs customers who receive an apparently used item to contact HOFISH about replacement.
