## Nursing access and support

- Release the clip at the strap to lower the front section for feeding. A built-in shelf bra provides an inner support layer.
- The current selections are unpadded. The product description suggests wearing them as supplied or adding your own inserts for extra coverage.

## Length and layering

- The stretchy, seamless body has a longer hem for coverage around the waist and bump. The pull-on cut can be worn on its own or under another top.
- HOFISH shows the same garment during pregnancy for its longer, stretchy cut and after birth for clip-down feeding access.

## Material, care and choices

- The product details specify 90% nylon and 10% spandex, dry cleaning only, pull-on construction and imported origin.
- The size selector covers M to XXL. Color options include the gray/black/white three-pack and assortments in black, latte and chocolate brown.
