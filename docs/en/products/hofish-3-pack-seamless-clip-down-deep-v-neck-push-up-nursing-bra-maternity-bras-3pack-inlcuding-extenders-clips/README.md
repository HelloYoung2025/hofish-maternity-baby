## Neckline, cups and nursing access

- The triangular cups create a low V at the front. A support band under the bust and wider sides are integrated into the seamless, wire-free construction.
- Use the shoulder clip to lower one cup and fasten it again after nursing. Adjust the shoulder straps and hook-and-eye back separately. Product imagery also shows use with a wearable pump inside a cup, subject to fit.

## Fabric and pack contents

- The description lists 92% nylon and 8% spandex, with removable molded foam pads on the described version. The selector separately names a fixed-pad option in pink, black and beige.
- The product description lists three bras, three extra clips, three band extenders and two additional push-up pad pieces. Check the contents of the particular pack you select, especially the fixed-pad option.

## Size guide from the product page

- S fits US 32B–32DD and 34B–34C according to the product chart.
- M fits US 34D–34DD, 36B–36D and 38B–38C.
- L fits US 38D–38DD and 40B–40D.
- XL fits US 42B–42DD. The size selector includes XXL, for which the published text chart gives no conversion.
- HOFISH suggests considering the next size up during pregnancy or nursing. The published values use US band and cup labels.
