## Cut and construction

- This three-pack has fuller cups and wider side panels for coverage across the bust. The wire-free knit stretches across the cups, with more closely knit areas around them for support.
- Each cup contains a shaped foam pad that can be removed. The back fastens with hooks and eyes, and the shoulder straps adjust separately.

## Using the nursing clips

- Lift the clip away from the body, lower the cup, then reattach it after feeding. The clip is designed to be opened with one hand while wearing the bra.
- The page illustrates a strap clip that brings the shoulder straps closer together at the back. It also shows a wearable pump worn inside a cup. Check whether the cup has room for your particular device.

## Fabric and fit

- The stated composition is 92% nylon and 8% spandex. HOFISH describes a close-fitting band and suggests trying the larger size when between sizes or when a looser fit is preferred.
- The selector ranges from S to XXL. The material tab lists the fabric and construction details. Consult the garment label for care.
