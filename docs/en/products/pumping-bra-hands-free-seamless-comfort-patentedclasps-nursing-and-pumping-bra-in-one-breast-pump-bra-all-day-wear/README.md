## Two-layer design

- The opening in the inner layer is shaped like a cross to hold a pump flange. Lifting or lowering the outer layer changes access for pumping or nursing.
- The multi-position clips are intended for three configurations: a separate flange, a wearable pump inside the cup, or direct feeding with the cup lowered. Check space and fit with your own pump.

## Support and everyday wear

- The bra uses a lightweight, elastic knit with a smooth profile under clothing. Adjustable straps, a ribbed underband and side support let you adjust the fit and wear it through your daily routine.
- HOFISH presents the two-layer construction as flexible as breast fullness changes over the day.
