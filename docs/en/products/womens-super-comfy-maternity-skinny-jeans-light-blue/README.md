## Waist and pockets

- These pull-on maternity shorts combine stretch denim with a smooth knit panel that extends over the bump. The source lists the denim portion as low rise alongside its description of the higher knit panel.
- The front pocket openings are decorative. The back pockets are functional for small items.

## Material and care

- The listed fabric is 75% rayon, 22% nylon and 3% spandex. The brand describes the denim as elastic to make movement easier in everyday wear.
- Hand wash only, as specified in the product details.

## Color and fit

- The selector lists light blue, dark blue and a color called New Navy, in Small through XX-Large.
- Despite the older URL referring to skinny jeans, the page title and design description identify these as shorts. The separate full-length maternity jeans listing has its own sizes and color options.
