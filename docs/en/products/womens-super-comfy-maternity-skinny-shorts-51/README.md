## Waist and fit

- Style 51 is a fitted, pull-on denim short. The flexible knit panel sits over the bump to accommodate changes in fit.
- The details list the denim as low rise. The knit panel rises above the denim portion of the waist.

## Pockets, material and washing

- The front pockets are decorative and the back pockets can carry small items. The brand describes the stretch denim as suitable for casual everyday wear.
- The fabric is listed as 75% rayon, 22% nylon and 3% spandex. Hand wash only.

## Style 51 sizes and colors

- The size selector offers Small through XX-Large, with dark green, navy and dark gray listed.
