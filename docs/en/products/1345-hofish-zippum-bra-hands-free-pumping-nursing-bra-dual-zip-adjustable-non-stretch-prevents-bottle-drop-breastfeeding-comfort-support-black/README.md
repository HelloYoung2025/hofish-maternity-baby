## Pumping and nursing

- Two reinforced vertical zippers form separate left and right openings. Set each opening around its flange for hands-free pumping. The front zipper area is designed to have less give than the stretchy body fabric.
- The nursing clips work independently of the zippers. Release a clip to lower a cup for direct nursing while wearing the same bra.

## Fabric and care

- HOFISH describes a smooth, flexible three-layer fabric intended to stay breathable and dry quickly under everyday clothing.
- The description lists machine washing and reports a brand test of 25 washes at 40 °C. It gives no fiber percentages. Use the garment label for the full wash instructions.

## Fit and flange compatibility

- The listing offers black in M, L, XL and 2XL. Its description cites A–D cups and US bands 30–40 without mapping the ranges to individual garment sizes.
- HOFISH cites Spectra and Medela as flange examples and reports a maximum bottle load of about 450 g per side. The listing provides neither a model-by-model compatibility table nor a test protocol for that load. Check the shape and fit of your own flange and bottle.
