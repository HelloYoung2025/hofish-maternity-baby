## Switching between nursing and pumping

- Open the shoulder clip and lower the nursing cup for feeding. For pumping, align the flange with the opening between the overlapping fabric panels. The front has no zipper.
- The feature illustrations show both flanges seated in the opening and wearable collection cups inside the bra. HOFISH provides no model-by-model compatibility list.

## Support and adjustment

- A U-shaped back, full cups and wire-free construction give the bra the shape of an everyday model. The stretch knit is listed as 92% nylon and 8% spandex.
- Adjust the two shoulder straps and the four-position hook-and-eye back. The matching extender described by HOFISH adds up to 2 in (about 5 cm) to the band.
