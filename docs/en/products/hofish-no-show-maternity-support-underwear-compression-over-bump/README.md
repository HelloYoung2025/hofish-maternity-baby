## Over-bump cut

- The high waist extends over the bump. The stretchy belly section is built into the brief as a single piece rather than a separate band.
- The front and edges use a seamless construction for a smooth profile under clothes. The cut provides coverage over the belly and across the lower back.

## Fabric and lining

- The Shopify product description lists 24% spandex in the stretch fabric and 100% cotton for the lining. It does not specify the remaining fibers in the outer fabric.
- No washing instructions are given in the product description. Follow the instructions on the garment label.

## Sizes and pack choices

- The selector lists S through 2XL and two- or three-brief packs. Combinations include black/beige, black/pink, two beige briefs, and beige/black/pink.
- Some two-packs are labeled as new in the selector. The description does not identify a construction change for those options.
